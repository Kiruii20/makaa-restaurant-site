The kitchen

Makaa is Swahili for charcoal. Everything here touches it.

We cook goat, fish and vegetables over a single charcoal grill and let the fire set the pace. Tamarind, pilipili and lemon do the rest.

The menu is short on purpose. When the market has nothing worth cooking, a dish comes off.
